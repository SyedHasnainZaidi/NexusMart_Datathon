1. Principles

  • All cleaning is code (nexusmart_etl/clean.py); nothing was edited by hand, in Excel, in the database or in Power Query.
  • Nothing is silently deleted. Every row that leaves a table is written to the reject table with a reason code. Value repairs are flagged on the row (dq_flags column in FactSales, *_imputed / *_repaired columns in staging).
  • Repair from evidence first (other columns or tables of the same record), impute second, quarantine only if neither is possible.
  • Thresholds are documented in code and in this report; the Airflow DAG fails when a critical quality threshold is breached.

4. Key decisions and assumptions

  • Dates. Slashed dates are day/month/year (brief). Order dates that are impossible or outside 1 Jan 2024 - 30 Jun 2025 (1,605 orders) were repaired from the earliest payment or shipment date of the same order (validated on clean orders: same month in 97% of cases, median error 0 days). 0 orders could not be repaired.
  • Unit price. Selling prices follow list price x a smooth inflation index (+0.25% per month, fitted on clean lines). Prices ~100x the expected value were divided by 100; negative prices made positive; prices in USD, zero, or 9,999,999 were solved exactly from order_total when that line was the only unknown in its order (927 lines), otherwise list price x index (16 lines).
  • Quantity. Words and "2.0" converted; negatives made positive; 0 or >50 (the 500 entry error) solved from order_total (627 lines) or set to 1 (14 lines).
  • Discounts. The order's discount_code is the authoritative %. Line discount = % x line gross when blank (31,567), larger than the line (871) or inconsistent (1). After all fixes only 7 orders differ from their order_total by more than 1%.
  • Duplicate customers (2,500 merged, 45,000 → 42,500): same normalised name AND same e-mail or phone, and no conflicting birth year or city. Never on name alone. Orders are re-pointed through dq.customer_id_map.
  • Duplicate products (25 merged): same name and category AND same cost or weight AND list price within 5%. Mapping kept in dq.product_id_map.
  • Broken references are mapped to the Unknown member (key -1) instead of being dropped, so revenue is kept: 932 lines with product IDs not in the catalogue, 321 orders with unknown customers. Return IDs with lost zero-padding were re-padded (139).
  • Ship city. Blank ship city filled with the customer's registered city (6,082 orders, flagged; 89% of orders ship to the registered city). The flag lets the dashboard exclude them.
  • Returns. Only non-Rejected (incl. "Declined") returns are valid. Refunds above the line's net value are capped at it (321). Returns against cancelled lines are quarantined (15).
  • Shipping. Missing, negative or 9,999,999 fees were imputed with the median fee of the same courier and order-weight band (9,311 orders). Delivery durations that are negative (1,269) or >30 days (266) are set to NULL, not guessed. One fee per order is allocated to its lines by gross-value share.
  • Products. Cost above 1.2x list price (an injected 1.5x pattern, 67 products) and blank costs (170) were re-imputed from the sub-category's median cost/list ratio. Invalid list prices were re-derived from the product's own sales.

8. Known limitations

  • One duplicate product pair (ElecPro Power Strip U248, P02216/P03090) is not merged: both records carry corrupted costs and one has no weight, so the rule cannot confirm the match without using the name alone.
  • June 2025 return rate (≈4%) is understated because some June orders are still inside their return window.
  • For 932 lines whose product is not in the catalogue, cost is estimated at 60% of the selling price (catalogue median).
  • Payment records exist for every order, including cancelled COD orders, and 4,815 orders have split payments; the order's payment method is the one with the largest amount.
